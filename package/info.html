Windows 媒体控制Windows media control

SMTC Music

在 HoloCubic 上显示 Windows 当前播放、专辑图、进度与同步歌词，并通过按键控制播放。 Shows Windows now-playing metadata, album art, progress, and synced lyrics on HoloCubic, with key-based playback controls.

功能亮点Highlights

  • 01SMTC 状态显示SMTC status

    读取 Windows 当前媒体会话，展示歌名、歌手、播放状态和进度。Reads the active Windows media session for title, artist, playback state, and timeline.

  • 02多来源歌词Multi-source lyrics

    已拆分网易云和 QQ 音乐适配，后续可继续添加新的音乐源。NetEase and QQ Music adapters are separated so more music sources can be added later.

  • 03专辑图优化Cover rendering

    对远端封面做设备尺寸适配，避免 QQ 音乐封面重复、偏移或裁切异常。Normalizes remote cover art for the device to avoid duplicated, shifted, or badly cropped QQ Music covers.

  • 04按键控制Key controls

    支持播放暂停、上一首、下一首、前进和后退 15 秒。Supports play or pause, previous, next, and 15-second seek controls.
